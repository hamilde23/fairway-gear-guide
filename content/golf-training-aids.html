Training aids give you a specific task or feedback signal during practice. Start with the skill you want to work on, and choose a tool that helps you understand that movement. No aid guarantees lower scores.

2. Swing Analyzers

Devices like Swingbyte provide real‑time data on swing speed, path and other metrics. They help you make immediate adjustments and refine your technique. Shop now.

3. Weighted Clubs

4. Alignment Sticks

Alignment sticks are simple but effective tools that ensure proper alignment and posture. Use them on the range to check your setup and swing path. Shop now.

5. Impact Bags

Impact bags help you practice the correct impact position by providing feedback when you strike the bag. Repetition builds muscle memory and ensures a square clubface at impact. Shop now.

6. Putting Mirrors

7. Tempo Trainers
