Training aids give you a specific task or feedback signal during practice. Start with the skill you want to work on, and choose a tool that helps you understand that movement. No aid guarantees lower scores.

This wearable aid is designed to encourage your arms and torso to move together. Use it to explore swing connection, rather than treating it as an injury-prevention device. Browse on Amazon: Golf Swing Shirt (opens in a new tab) ↗.

3. Weighted Clubs

5. Impact Bags

Impact bags help you practice the correct impact position by providing feedback when you strike the bag. Repetition builds muscle memory and ensures a square clubface at impact. Browse on Amazon: Impact Bags (opens in a new tab) ↗.
