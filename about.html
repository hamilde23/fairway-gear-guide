I’m Derek—a Pacific Northwest golfer with a curiosity for equipment, courses and the next great golf trip.

Why Fairway Gear Guide exists

Golf equipment can be expensive and confusing. I started Fairway Gear Guide to make it easier to explore the options, choose what fits your game and discover places worth playing.

I live in the Pacific Northwest and enjoy golf, travel and BBQ. When I’m not exploring a course or looking into gear, I’m planning another road trip or working on my brisket.

How to read our guides

Our collection includes equipment overviews and course guides. An overview is not the same as a measured, head-to-head product test. Existing guides do not include a full testing protocol or original performance data, so use them as a starting point—not a substitute for a fitting.

Course ratings are subjective. Older prices and restaurant suggestions are labeled as archive information and should be checked directly before booking.

How affiliate links work

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you buy through an affiliate link, I may receive a commission. The Amazon links in these guides lead to search results; prices, sellers and availability can change.
